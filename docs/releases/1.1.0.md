## What's new

- Weight charts can show weeks or calendar dates (Weeks / Dates above each chart)
- Light and dark mode: a button in the top bar, or Settings › Appearance (System, Light, Dark)
- BMI labels no longer overlap when your start and goal are close
- Works better in narrow windows, and the calorie schedule ends with what to eat at your goal

Your plan and weigh-ins are kept when you update. Install it from the update banner in Weightmaxer 1.0.0 (Download, then Install and restart), or download `Weightmaxer-win-x64.zip` and replace `Weightmaxer.exe`. The executable is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first start.
