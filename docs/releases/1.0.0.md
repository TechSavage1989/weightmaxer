## What's new

- Plan to lose or gain weight by pace or by target date, with a goal date and daily calorie target
- Choose your own lowest daily calories (down to 800 kcal), with a warning below the standard minimum
- Daily protein, fat and carb targets in three diet styles
- Log weigh-ins and compare them with your plan
- Update banner with Download and Install and restart, checked and verified before installing

Weightmaxer is a portable 64-bit Windows app: unzip `Weightmaxer-win-x64.zip` and start `Weightmaxer.exe`. Check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` if you like. The executable is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first start.
