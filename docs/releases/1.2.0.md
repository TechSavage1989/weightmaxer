## What's new

- Point at a weight chart to see the date and the planned weight on that day
- On the Weigh-ins chart, the readout also shows your weigh-in from that day or one within 3 days
- Works with a mouse, by touching and dragging on a phone, or with the arrow keys after clicking the chart

Your plan and weigh-ins are kept when you update. Install it from the update banner (Download, then Install and restart), or download `Weightmaxer-win-x64.zip` and replace `Weightmaxer.exe`. The executable is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm the first start.
